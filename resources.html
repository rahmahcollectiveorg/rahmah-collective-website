Lactation Support

You are not alone in this journey

Breastfeeding is one of the most beautiful manifestations of the deep bond between a mother and her child. However, breastfeeding does not come naturally for everyone. There are a range of obstacles a mother and baby could experience in their breastfeeding journey, but know that these are common and you are not alone. Below are some trusted resources that may help you on your journey and allow you to enjoy this special connection with your little one.

Educational Resources

  • KellyMom.com — Visit site →
  • La Leche League — breastfeeding info — Visit site →
