About Us

Mission, story, and why it matters

Our Mission

Our mission is to support maternal health and reproductive care by providing the Islamically grounded education, community and data-driven resources that women deserve at every stage of their journey to and through motherhood.

Our Vision

A world where every Muslim mother has the knowledge, community, and care she needs to thrive.

Our Story

Why "Rahmah"?

As an organization supporting Muslim women and mothers, Rahmah (رحمة) Collective earned its name because of the extraordinary depth in its Arabic root: the same root that gives us al-Rahman, al-Raheem, and Ra'him: the womb. Mercy and the womb, inseparably connected.
