About Us

Mission, story, and why it matters

Our Mission

Rahmah Collective aims to support maternal health and reproductive care by providing Islamically grounded education, community, and data-based research at every stage in a woman's journey to and through motherhood.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a one-stop shop for women to be spiritually and holistically supported in their many eras of womanhood and motherhood.

Our Story

Why "Rahmah"?

As an organization supporting Muslim women and mothers, Rahmah (رحمة) Collective earned its name because of the extraordinary depth in its Arabic root: the same root that gives us al-Rahman, al-Raheem, and Ra'him: the womb. Mercy and the womb, inseparably connected.

Our Work

Initiatives, research, and programs

Everything we build falls into three pillars: learning, community, and evidence, each shaping the others.

  • Pre-motherhood classes
  • Maternal physical therapy workshop
  • OBGYN & preventative health seminar
  • Fertility seminar
  • Lactation during Ramadan seminar
  • Workshops on PPD, PPA & mommy burnout, with a therapist and an Alimah
  • Infographics & pamphlets
  • Barakah Cycle: a library-style system of gifting and lending. After gentle use, items return to us to stock and re-gift, giving each item multiple lives and supporting one another as a shared village. Request, Gift, or Lend an Item
  • Rahmah community-wide group chat & ask-anything forum
  • Mom & Tot Yoga, Pilates
  • Swimming lessons
  • Rahmah Baby Pod chats: Summer '25–'26 & Summer '26–'27 babies
  • Take our surveys to help shape resources and research for our community. Take a survey →
  • Provider review database: a community-sourced directory of trusted providers. Coming soon
