Where Your Gift Goes

Three ways your support takes shape

Education

Funds seminars, workshops, and printed resources, ranging from pre-motherhood classes to PPD/PPA support led by a therapist and an Alimah together.

Community

Restocks the Barakah Cycle lending library, and supports Mom & Tot classes, swimming lessons, and our Baby Pod chats.

Data & Research

Powers our community surveys and the provider review database, building the evidence base for better maternal care.
